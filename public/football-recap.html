Liberty Center Tigers Football Recap

Friday, November 6, 2026 · OHSAA Division VI, Region 22 Quarterfinal

Liberty Center 45, Paulding 10

The Liberty Center Tigers continued their unbeaten 2026 season with a convincing 45–10 home playoff victory over the Paulding Panthers. The Tigers scored 17 points in the first quarter and 21 more in the second to take a commanding 38–7 lead into halftime. Liberty Center added seven in the third quarter and held Paulding scoreless in the fourth.

The victory moved Liberty Center to 11–0 and sent the Tigers to the regional semifinals. Next up: Columbus Grove on Friday, November 13, 2026, at 7:00 p.m.
