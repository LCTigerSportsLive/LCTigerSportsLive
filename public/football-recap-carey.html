Liberty Center Tigers Football Recap

Friday, November 20, 2026 · OHSAA Division VI, Region 22 Final

Liberty Center 42, Carey 0

The Tigers won the Region 22 title at Lima Senior’s Spartan Stadium, improving to 13–0. Liberty Center led 35–0 at halftime and outgained Carey 405–153.

Game standouts: Garrison Kruse rushed for 165 yards on 11 carries, with touchdowns of 67 and 50 yards. Ethan Gray ran five times for 71 yards, including a 45-yard score. Jack Badenhop completed 4 of 7 passes for 114 yards and two touchdowns, finding Isaiah Badenhop and Cole Spangler.

Season team totals: Liberty Center has outscored opponents 665–90, averaging 51.2 points scored and 6.9 allowed per game, with four shutouts. Updated individual season totals and complete defensive game statistics were unavailable.

Next game: Liberty Center faces St. Henry (11–3) in the Division VI state semifinal Friday, November 27, at 7 p.m. Eastern. The Tigers are the designated home team; the neutral-site venue was still to be announced as of November 21.
