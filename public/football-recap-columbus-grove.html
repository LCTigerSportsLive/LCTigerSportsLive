Liberty Center Tigers Football Recap

Friday, November 13, 2026 · OHSAA Division VI, Region 22 Semifinal

Liberty Center 51, Columbus Grove 6

The Liberty Center Tigers delivered a dominant playoff performance Friday night, rolling past Columbus Grove 51–6 at Liberty Center. Facing a Bulldogs squad that entered with a 10–1 record, the Tigers controlled the regional semifinal and won by 45 points.

The victory keeps Liberty Center unbeaten at 12–0 and sends the Tigers to the Division VI, Region 22 championship game. Columbus Grove finishes its season at 10–2.

Next game: Liberty Center vs. Carey, Friday, November 20, 2026, in the Region 22 final. The game site and kickoff details should be confirmed with OHSAA when announced.
