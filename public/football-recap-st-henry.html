Liberty Center Tigers Football Recap

Friday, November 27, 2026 · OHSAA Division VI State Semifinal

Liberty Center 17, St. Henry 0

Liberty Center moved to 14–0 and returned to the state championship game with a shutout at Sidney Memorial Stadium. Max Perry kicked a 21-yard field goal just before halftime to give the Tigers a 3–0 lead.

Game standouts: Garrison Kruse ran for two second-half touchdowns and topped 200 yards rushing. Reports differ on his exact total: The Blade reported 217 yards, while NewsForce247 reported 213. Ethan Gray intercepted three passes, and Liberty Center held St. Henry to 132 total yards.

Season statistics: Through 14 games, Liberty Center has outscored opponents 682–90, averaging 48.7 points scored and 6.4 allowed, with five shutouts. Entering the semifinal, quarterback Jack Badenhop had 1,360 passing yards and 16 passing touchdowns, plus 263 rushing yards and six rushing touchdowns. Updated individual season totals through the semifinal weren’t verified.

Next game: Liberty Center faces South Range (13–1) in the Division VI state championship on Saturday, December 5, at 3 p.m. Eastern at Tom Benson Hall of Fame Stadium in Canton. It’s a neutral site, with Liberty Center designated as the home team.
